## Q2 端到端加密团队协作产品的密钥管理与恢复

### 2.1 1Password Business（主依据：官方安全白皮书 PDF，文件创建/修改时间 2024-12-10）

来源【一手】：https://1passwordstatic.com/files/security/1password-white-paper.pdf （PDF 元数据 CreationDate/ModDate = 2024-12-10；抓取于 2026-10-09，本地用 PDFKit 抽取文本核对）

- 密钥位置："All cryptographic keys are generated by the client on your devices, and all encryption is done locally"；"Server ignorance"——服务端不掌握账户密码与密钥；两秘密派生（2SKD）把本地 Secret Key 与账户密码混合，服务端存储的验证数据不可用于破解。（白皮书第 1 章）
- 团队恢复（Recovery Group，12.2–12.5 节）：
  - Owners/Organizers/Administrators 默认自动成为 Recovery Group 成员。
  - "When a vault is created, a copy of the vault key is encrypted with the public key of the recovery group"——从纯密码学角度 Recovery Group 成员可访问所有保险库。
  - "Recovery is recovery of the vault keys — it's not recovery of the account password or Secret Key"。
  - 防护靠服务端访问控制：恢复组成员拿不到其无权访问保险库的密文数据；只有在被恢复用户"has re-created their account"后服务器才下发加密的 vault key。
  - 风险提示：恢复请求应"verified independently of email (Face to face or a phone call)"；"If there are no members of a recovery group, the capacity to recover data is lost to the team"。
- 新设备加入（8.4 / 9.x 节）：
  - 密码账户：新设备需要"add-device link（可为二维码）"+ 账户密码 + Secret Key；邀请走邮件，白皮书提示邮件的安全局限、建议管理员线下核实。
  - Passkey/SSO 账户：每台设备生成不离开本机的 device key；新设备须"由已登记设备授权"——服务端通知所有已链接设备 → 旧设备生成 **6 位 setup code**，用 **CPace（PAKE）** 与新设备建立信道 → 旧设备把 credential bundle 加密后经服务器转发；"In the event a malicious server attempts to interfere in the key agreement process, 1Password clients detect the presence and abandon participation."
- **服务端能否注入/替换公钥（附录 C "Verifying public keys"）**：白皮书明文承认"it would be possible for a malicious or compromised 1Password server to provide dishonest public keys to the user and run a successful attack… acquire vault encryption keys with little ability for users to detect or prevent this"；并指出该威胁"probably most significant with respect to the automatic addition of vaults to the Recovery Group"。两类防御（信任层级/用户间指纹核对）均被称为"(poor) solution"，1Password 未实现强制公钥核验。另："One of the primary roles of the 1Password server is to ensure that public keys belong to the right email addresses… we have no information about who controls that email address."
- Secret Key / Emergency Kit【一手，经检索摘录】：Secret Key 为 34 位字符、128 bit 熵，"We have no record of your Secret Key and can't recover it"；Emergency Kit 含登录地址、邮箱、Secret Key、密码填写处与 Setup Code 二维码；管理员恢复后用户"get a new Secret Key and Emergency Kit"；另有"recovery code"可在忘记密码或丢失 Emergency Kit 时恢复。——https://support.1password.com/secret-key-security/ 、https://support.1password.com/emergency-kit/ （页面日期未取得）
- 【二手】1Password 社区帖讨论 ETH Zürich 论文对"服务端替换公钥"的分析。——https://www.1password.community/1password-at-home-31/eth-zuerich-paper-concerns-24042 （论文本身与 1Password 官方回应本轮未核到）

### 2.2 Bitwarden

- 【一手】Account Recovery（管理员恢复，原 Admin Password Reset）："When a member of the organization enrolls in account recovery, that user's encryption key is encrypted with the organization's public key. The result is stored as the Account Recovery Key"；恢复时：组织私钥由组织对称密钥解开 → 解出用户加密密钥 → 用新主密钥重新加密并生成新主密码哈希。权限：只有 admin/owner 能恢复 admin，只有 owner 能恢复 owner。可配置自动登记（automatic enrollment）策略。——https://bitwarden.com/help/account-recovery/ （页面未取得日期，抓取于 2026-10-09）
- 【一手】SSO + Trusted Devices（新设备免主密码）：新设备向服务器 Authentication Request 表提交"account email address and a unique auth-request public key"；管理员在 Device approvals 页批准后，"the approving client encrypts the user's account encryption key using the auth-request public key enclosed in the request"并 PUT 回该记录。——https://bitwarden.com/help/about-trusted-devices/ （抓取于 2026-10-09）
